Everything a TV night needs

Built for the remote first, smooth on every screen, and it keeps your details on your device.

Full TV guide

A smooth-scrolling grid of what's on now and next. Scroll back through earlier shows and catch up on channels that keep recordings.

Pause, rewind, scrub

Press OK while watching for a TiviMate-style player panel: scrub the timeline, pause, jump back to live.

Movies & series

Browse your provider's on-demand library by category, see seasons and episodes, and pick up where you left off.

Your playlist, your way

Rename it, fix guide times, renumber channels, pick the stream format or User-Agent, hide groups, order favorites.

Themes for every season

Everyday themes and holiday themes with their own scenery, from a moonlit Halloween graveyard to fireworks for New Year.

VPN and other players

On Fire TV: a built-in WireGuard VPN, and full screen can open in VLC or MX Player if you prefer them.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Download Nightdial.ipa on your computer.
  2. Install Sideloadly, plug in your iPhone, drag the file in, sign in with your Apple ID and press Start.
  3. On the iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Trust. Turn on Developer Mode if asked.

With a free Apple ID the app lasts 7 days. Sideloadly can refresh it for you.

Web

  1. In your browser: open the web player and sign in. Works with providers whose server uses https.
  2. On your computer: for providers on plain http (most of them), download the computer version, then run node server.js and open localhost:8080.

Browsers block plain-http servers on secure web pages, so the computer version passes them through for you.

Private by design

No sign-up, no tracking, nothing in between you and your provider.

Encrypted on your devicePasswords and playlist links are stored with Android Keystore on Fire TV and the Keychain on iPhone.
Only sent to your providerYour details go straight to the IPTV service you sign in to, and nowhere else.
No ads, no analyticsThis site and the apps load nothing from ad or tracking networks.
Locked downStrict content security rules: the player only runs its own code, can't be framed by other sites and sends no referrer.
Open sourceAll of it is on GitHub, and every app download is built there from that code.
